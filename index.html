Your privacy matters.

This policy explains how PitchUP handles information when you use our football games, bookings, hosting, and community features.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

PitchUP uses information to provide accounts, bookings, payments, player profiles, game communication, and notifications. Below, we explain these uses and how to contact us about your privacy.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the PitchUP app and related services. It describes information handled through accounts, profiles, game participation, bookings, payments, and communication.

For questions about this policy or how information is handled, contact support@pitchup.life.

2. Information we handle

  • Accounts: email address, account identifier, authentication information, and account settings.
  • Profiles: information you choose to provide, such as a display name, photo, playing position, playing level, city, biography, or social profile handle.
  • Games and bookings: games you join or host, booking status, cancellations, invitations, and player awards or nominations.
  • Communication: game chat messages, support requests, and information you provide when contacting us.
  • Transactions: payment and refund status, transaction references, and information needed to apply promotions.
  • Notifications: notification preferences and push tokens used to deliver notifications to your app installation.

Technical information may also be processed by the infrastructure used to provide the service, including connection and request information needed to operate and secure that infrastructure.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and sign-in.
  • To provide game listings, bookings, and hosting.
  • To process payments, promotions, and refunds.
  • To provide profiles, invitations, game chat, and awards.
  • To send booking updates and game or host reminders.
  • To send optional promotional updates according to your preferences and applicable requirements.
  • To respond to questions and investigate service problems.
  • To protect the service, address misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Visibility and sharing

PitchUP includes community features. Information you add to a player profile may be visible to other players. Participation and hosting information may appear in game-related views.

Game chat messages are shared with people who have access to that chat. Avoid posting sensitive information in profiles or messages.

We use service providers to operate PitchUP, including:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and backend services.
  • Stripe: payment processing and related transaction services.
  • Expo, Apple, and Google: push notification delivery where applicable.
  • Resend: delivery of app-related emails.

Providers process information necessary for their functions. Their own privacy notices describe processing for which they are independently responsible.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to investigate misuse, protect people, or establish or defend legal claims.

5. Payments

PitchUP uses Stripe for supported card payment flows. When you use these flows, payment details are handled through Stripe's payment systems.

PitchUP uses transaction references and payment status to manage bookings, promotions, refunds, and payment support. Stripe's privacy policy explains its own handling of payment-related information.

Read Stripe's privacy policy

6. Permissions and notification choices

You can manage notification preferences within PitchUP and control app notification permission through your device settings.

Where a feature requests access to location, photos, or the camera, you can allow or decline that permission and change it later in device settings. Features that depend on a permission may not be available if it is declined.

A device permission is not, by itself, an explanation of whether information is stored or shared. Please contact us if you need clarification about a particular feature.

7. Retention and security

Information is retained for the purposes described in this policy, taking account of whether your account remains active, outstanding bookings or support matters, dispute handling, and legal requirements.

Some transaction or dispute-related records may need to be retained after an account is deleted. Backup copies may remain until they expire under the applicable backup lifecycle.

We use safeguards intended to protect information. No online service or method of storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. Account deletion and privacy requests

You can contact support@pitchup.life to request account deletion or ask about information associated with your account.

We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder before acting. Where information must be retained for legal obligations or dispute handling, we will explain the applicable limitations.

Signing out or uninstalling PitchUP does not automatically delete your account.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information; restrict or object to processing; and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights may be subject to conditions and exceptions.

Contact us to exercise your rights. You may also raise a complaint with the data protection authority responsible for your region.

10. Processing locations

Providers used by PitchUP may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable protections depend on the service, processing location, and relevant legal requirements.

Contact us for information about the providers, processing locations, and transfer safeguards relevant to your request.

11. Policy updates

We may update this policy as PitchUP's features, practices, or legal obligations change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice.
